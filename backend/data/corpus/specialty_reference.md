# Medical Specialty Reference — Symptom Mapping

## Cardiology
Symptoms: chest pain, chest tightness, palpitations, irregular heartbeat, shortness of breath on exertion, leg swelling, syncope, hypertension, racing heart, rapid heartbeat.
Conditions: angina, myocardial infarction, arrhythmia, heart failure, hypertensive crisis.
MTS correlation: Level 1–2 for acute chest pain; Level 3–4 for chronic cardiac symptoms.

## Neurology
Symptoms: severe headache (thunderclap), dizziness, vertigo, numbness or tingling in limbs, facial asymmetry, speech difficulty, vision changes, weakness in one side, loss of balance, confusion, memory loss, seizures, blurred vision, visual disturbances, frequent headaches, migraine, light sensitivity.
Conditions: stroke, TIA, migraine, epilepsy, multiple sclerosis, neuropathy.
MTS correlation: Level 2 for acute stroke signs; Level 3 for severe migraine; Level 4 for chronic neurological complaints.

## Gastroenterology
Symptoms: severe abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, blood in stool, heartburn, difficulty swallowing, bloating, jaundice, burning sensation in abdomen, burning sensation in throat, indigestion, belching, sour or bitter taste in mouth, dark tarry stools, constipation, painful bowel movements, anal pain, anal inflammation, hard stool, bloody stools.
Conditions: appendicitis, peptic ulcer, peptic ulcer disease, gastritis, irritable bowel syndrome, cholecystitis, pancreatitis, gastroesophageal reflux disease, hemorrhoids.
MTS correlation: Level 2 for peritoneal signs; Level 3 for persistent vomiting; Level 4 for mild gastrointestinal complaints.

## Orthopedics
Symptoms: bone pain, joint swelling, limited range of motion, back pain, neck pain, muscle weakness, deformity after injury, inability to bear weight, joint pain, joint stiffness, neck stiffness, difficulty moving, walking pain, muscle cramps, leg cramps.
Conditions: fractures, sprains, arthritis, disc herniation, tendinopathy, cervical spondylosis.
MTS correlation: Level 3 for suspected fractures; Level 4 for minor sprains; Level 3 for back pain with neurological deficit.

## Pulmonology
Symptoms: persistent cough, shortness of breath at rest, wheezing, coughing blood, night sweats, unexplained weight loss with respiratory symptoms, thick mucus, phlegm production, rusty coloured phlegm, rapid heartbeat with cough, difficulty breathing, chest tightness.
Conditions: asthma exacerbation, bronchial asthma, COPD, pneumonia, pulmonary embolism, tuberculosis.
MTS correlation: Level 2 for severe dyspnoea; Level 3 for moderate respiratory distress; Level 4 for stable asthma.

## Internal Medicine / General Practice
Symptoms: undifferentiated complaints, systemic illness, fever without clear source, general malaise, fatigue, multi-system symptoms not fitting a specific specialty, high fever, chills, sweating, headache, muscle pain, loss of appetite, weight loss, increased thirst, frequent urination, dry mouth, increased appetite, dizziness, body ache.
Conditions: viral infections (common cold), systemic inflammatory conditions, metabolic disorders (diabetes), unexplained symptoms, malaria, dengue, typhoid.
MTS correlation: Level 2–3 for high fever with systemic symptoms; Level 4 for mild constitutional symptoms.

## Urology
Symptoms: pain on urination, blood in urine, kidney/flank pain, difficulty urinating, frequent urination with burning, testicular pain, frequent urges to urinate with little output, cloudy or bloody urine, foul-smelling urine, pelvic pain, burning sensation during urination.
Conditions: urinary tract infection, kidney stones, pyelonephritis, urinary retention.
MTS correlation: Level 2 for severe renal colic; Level 3 for UTI with fever; Level 4 for lower urinary tract symptoms.

## Dermatology
Symptoms: rash, skin lesions, itching, blistering, skin colour changes, skin peeling, dry scaly patches, silver scales, nail pitting, thickened skin, cracked skin, bleeding skin, inflamed skin, discoloured skin, nodular eruptions, raised lumps, darkened spots, pus-filled pimples, blackheads, skin scarring, sensitive skin, fluid-filled lesions, yellow ooze, crusting sores, red bumpy rash.
Conditions: contact dermatitis, psoriasis, eczema, cellulitis, urticaria, fungal infection, acne, impetigo, chicken pox.
MTS correlation: Level 2 for anaphylactic rash with systemic symptoms; Level 4–5 for isolated skin complaints.

## Psychiatry
Symptoms: acute agitation, suicidal ideation, self-harm, severe anxiety, psychotic symptoms, acute confusion, difficulty concentrating, insomnia.
MTS correlation: Level 2 for immediate risk; Level 3 for acute psychiatric crisis.

## ENT (Otolaryngology)
Symptoms: ear pain, hearing loss, tinnitus, severe sore throat, difficulty swallowing (throat related), persistent nosebleed (epistaxis), nasal congestion, hoarseness, sneezing, runny nose, cough, congestion.
Conditions: otitis media, tonsillitis, peritonsillar abscess, sinusitis, epistaxis, common cold, allergy.
MTS correlation: Level 2 for airway compromise or uncontrollable epistaxis; Level 4 for mild ear pain or sore throat.

## Ophthalmology
Symptoms: sudden vision loss, eye pain, severe eye redness, double vision (diplopia), flashes of light, floaters, chemical exposure to eye, blurred vision, visual disturbances, itchy eyes, watery eyes, light sensitivity.
Conditions: acute angle-closure glaucoma, retinal detachment, conjunctivitis, corneal abrasion, migraine (visual aura).
MTS correlation: Level 2 for sudden vision loss or chemical burns; Level 3 for severe eye pain; Level 4 for mild redness/itching.

## Gynecology
Symptoms: severe pelvic pain, abnormal vaginal bleeding, pregnancy-related pain or bleeding, severe menstrual cramps, vaginal discharge.
Conditions: ectopic pregnancy, miscarriage, pelvic inflammatory disease, ovarian torsion.
MTS correlation: Level 1 for eclampsia; Level 2 for severe bleeding or suspected ectopic pregnancy; Level 4 for mild pelvic pain.

## General Surgery
Symptoms: severe localized abdominal pain with rebound tenderness, acute trauma, vomiting blood, severe localized swelling or abscess.
Conditions: appendicitis, bowel obstruction, perforated ulcer, acute cholecystitis, trauma.
MTS correlation: Level 1–2 for acute surgical abdomen or major trauma; Level 3 for localized abscess.

## Vascular Surgery
Symptoms: prominent visible veins, leg cramps, bruising on legs, leg rash, leg discomfort, swelling, fatigue, visible blood vessels, skin changes on legs.
Conditions: varicose veins, chronic venous insufficiency, deep vein thrombosis.
MTS correlation: Level 3 for acute DVT suspicion; Level 4–5 for chronic varicose veins.

## Toxicology / Pharmacology
Symptoms: metallic taste in mouth, change of taste or smell, joint pain, muscle pain, headache, tremors, muscle twitching, fever, dizziness, confusion, racing heart, insomnia, nausea, skin rash, facial swelling.
Conditions: drug reaction, adverse drug effects, medication toxicity.
MTS correlation: Level 2 for anaphylaxis or severe reaction; Level 3 for moderate adverse effects; Level 4 for mild drug intolerance.

## Endocrinology
Symptoms: increased thirst, frequent urination, dry mouth, increased hunger, blurred vision, fatigue, dizziness, weight changes, sweating, tremors.
Conditions: diabetes mellitus, thyroid disorders, metabolic syndrome.
MTS correlation: Level 2 for diabetic emergencies; Level 3 for new-onset symptoms; Level 4 for stable metabolic complaints.

## Infectious Disease
Symptoms: high fever, chills, severe joint pain, skin rash, intense itching, vomiting, headache, loss of appetite, red spots on skin, back pain, muscle pain, fatigue, night sweats, weight loss, abdominal pain, constipation, diarrhea, bloody stools, cough, breathing difficulty.
Conditions: malaria, dengue, typhoid, tuberculosis, chicken pox.
MTS correlation: Level 2 for severe systemic infection or hemorrhagic fever; Level 3 for febrile illness with focal symptoms; Level 4 for mild self-limiting infections.
